# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our standards

- Be respectful and welcoming to people of every background and experience
  level.
- Give and receive constructive feedback with care.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

The following are not acceptable:

- harassment, personal or political attacks, and trolling
- sexualized language or imagery
- publishing other people's private information, including API keys
- any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and
other channels where someone represents the project.

Report incidents privately to the maintainer through the contact options at
<https://github.com/tolga-ileri>. Reports are kept confidential.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions. They may also
temporarily or permanently ban contributors whose behavior violates this
code.
